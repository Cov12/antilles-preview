Product case study

Job Description Express

A job-description platform built for K-12 HR teams, now with a built-in AI assistant that helps write new job descriptions and improve existing ones, while HR stays in control of every final version.

Request a Demo

Use case

From role details to a finished, consistent job description.

Job Description Express gives HR one place to create, manage, and publish job descriptions for teacher, administrator, and support-staff roles, with a standard structure across the district and control over how each description is displayed.

Who it serves

K-12 HR teams that manage role descriptions and hiring documentation across many positions and departments.

What it replaces

Scattered templates, copied files, and job descriptions that drift in format and quality from one department to the next.

Why it matters

Clear, consistent job descriptions make hiring, evaluation, and public posting easier, and the AI assistant helps keep the whole library at that standard.

AI assistant

AI that works inside your district's format, not around it.

The assistant is built into the screens HR already uses. It learns from your district's existing job descriptions and follows your format, so its output looks like it came from your office.

Improve with AI

Open any job description and get a refined version in place. The assistant tightens wording, splits run-on duties into clear single-responsibility bullets, uses consistent action verbs, and keeps the district's section structure intact. Each change comes with a short explanation, and potential issues (such as an item in the wrong section) are flagged for review instead of silently changed. A live progress indicator shows where the request stands.

Create with AI

Start from a job title. The assistant checks the district's existing job descriptions and standard occupational data for a starting point, then guides you through the role in a short conversation. Classification questions can be answered or skipped, and the finished draft opens directly in the editor, ready to review.

HR stays in control

The assistant never invents duties or qualifications, and it never sets or changes legally significant details like classification, FLSA status, salary, or retirement plan. Those remain HR's decisions. Nothing is published until HR accepts, edits, or rejects the suggestion.

What's included

Everything HR needs to manage job descriptions.

  • Standardized structure for teacher, administrator, and support-staff roles
  • AI-assisted improvement of existing job descriptions
  • AI-guided creation of new job descriptions
  • Control over final display settings
  • Public access to active job descriptions
  • Dedicated deployment for each district
